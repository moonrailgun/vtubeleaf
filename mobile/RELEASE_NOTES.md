VTubeLeaf Phone for Android turns your phone into a face tracking camera for VTubeLeaf on your computer.

- Scan the pairing QR code in VTubeLeaf with both devices on the same Wi-Fi.
- Requires Android 8.0 or later. Download and install the APK below.
- If you previously installed a debug build, uninstall it before installing this signed release. Later signed releases can be installed as updates.

---

VTubeLeaf Phone 安卓版可将手机变成电脑上 VTubeLeaf 的面捕摄像头。

- 手机与电脑连接同一 Wi-Fi，在手机端扫描电脑上的配对二维码。
- 支持 Android 8.0 及以上，下载下方 APK 安装。
- 若之前安装过调试版，需先卸载再安装此正式签名版本；之后的正式版本可覆盖升级。
